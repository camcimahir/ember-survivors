The sidearm doesn't count

Every run also starts with a Steel Sidearm that fires at the nearest enemy. It never appears on a card and costs no element slot — it exists so you are never completely without a way to fight back. It scales with passives, not with levels.

Elemental reactions

6 Automatic — every weapon benefits

These are the passive half of the combination system. They are resolved on every point of damage dealt, so any weapon carrying the right element gets them for free — no fusion required. This is why some element pairs feel strong together long before Level 3.

Steel → Frozen ×2.0

Steel shatters frozen enemies for double damage. The single biggest reaction in the game.

Storm → Wet / Chilled ×1.5

Storm conducts through anything frost has soaked or slowed.

Fire → Wet ×1.35

Fire flashes the water off for a burst, consuming the soak in the process.

Fire → Frozen ×1.3

Fire thaws the target, trading most of the freeze duration for extra damage.

Verdant → Marked ×1.2

Poison feeds on targets a void rift has marked.

Void mark → Everything +mark

A void mark amplifies all incoming damage from every source, not just void.
